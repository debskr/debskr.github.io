Aetheria

Aetheria is an atmospheric desktop weather dashboard designed to push the boundaries of glassmorphism and real-time environmental visual physics using Google Stitch. Treating the interface not as flat panels, but as a physical layer of poured liquid glass floating over dynamic skies, the UI organically reacts to live meteorological data with physical refraction, light scattering, and ambient shadows.

The system models physical weather states across distinct meteorological environments: clear skies cast volumetric sunlight with warm elongated panel shadows, rain and storms trigger micro-water droplet accumulation with refractive beading physics and periodic lightning flashes, overcast states render drifting multi-layer volumetric clouds with parallax depth, and night states display twinkling star fields with cosmic shooting stars.

Created as an experimental design system exploration for the Contra x Google Stitch Challenge, Aetheria leverages custom token design libraries, Gemini 3.1 Pro iterative variant branching across 6 screens, and fine-tuned CSS backdrop filters (40px blur, 200% saturation, specular highlights) to deliver an immersive, interactive atmospheric experience.
