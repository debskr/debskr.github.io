AI News Explorer

AI News Explorer is a platform for showcasing the latest AI-based applications, tools, and innovations. With current AI tech advancements, it is really difficult to track down and get information quickly. AI News Explorer is the single platform that indexes the latest innovations in different AI domains and lists them for better search and quick reviews.

I have researched and indexed all the relevant news and data using JSON, and vibe coded the entire application to utilize and manage the data effectively. The platform features smart filtering and search capabilities, enabling users to quickly find the most relevant AI news and developments that match their interests and professional needs.
