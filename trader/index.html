About My Trading Journey

My trading journey started on TradingView, where I spent countless hours decoding price charts and understanding how buyers and sellers drive market trends. After mastering fundamental tools like RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands, I realized the need for more adaptable solutions. I taught myself PineScript and began developing custom indicators tailored to identify healthy trends while filtering out false signals in sideways markets. As my indicators grew in accuracy, I successfully published my top-performing tools to the TradingView community.

Driven by a passion for systematic trading, I transitioned from manual analysis to full-scale algorithmic automation. Leveraging my background in software development, I translated my most profitable strategies into C# cBots for cTrader. To ensure mathematical rigor, I backtested these systems against 10 years of historical data using genetic algorithms for optimal parameter tuning.

Today, my best-performing strategies run autonomously via MQL5 scripts hosted on Azure VPS servers, delivering real-time forward trading alerts. Beyond chart analysis, I have also engineered custom web applications that scrape global financial data to gauge market sentiment, providing a complete, data-driven edge in the modern Forex ecosystem.

TrendFire

Trend Fire is one of the trend following strategy amongst many strategies out there. But the unique part of Trend Fire lies in the implementation and its accuracy to identify healthy Trends. Trend Fire is a purely Mathematical Indicator and aims for generating more successful trade signals. It has a unique strategy to avoid sideways market, false signals, and calculation to find entry for Trends, hence, more quality of trades.

Volatility Adaptive MA

Volatility adaptive moving average always reacts to the price and creates a larger distance/gap with price when there’s volatility in the market. Otherwise, it moves close with price in a sideways market. This nature of adaptability helps to reduce more false trades and indicate perfect scenarios to enter into market to take profitable trades.

Trendfire Strength Meter

Trend Fire Strength Meter (TFSM) focuses on detecting sideways trend and high volatility trend on market cycle. Market always moves by creating cycles of bullish and bearish trend but not every trend strength is equal. Thus, trends in sideways market cycle will not be profitable. TFSM helps to detect the trend strength though strength of volatility of any cycle.

Lovi Scalper

Lovi Scalper indicator is a dynamic trading tool designed to identify potential breakout opportunities and track active trends. It plots adaptive channel lines based on recent price action. When the price breaks out of these channels with sufficient momentum and meets a trend strength criterion, the indicator generates BUY or SELL entry signals. It also provides visual cues for trade exits and includes a performance dashboard to track pip gains/losses.

Swing Cycles Pro

This indicator is a dynamic breakout and trend-following system. Instead of using static price levels or standard moving averages, it builds dynamic support and resistance lines based on historical price extremes (Swing Highs and Swing Lows) that coincide with momentum extremes (RSI Overbought and Oversold conditions). It looks to enter trades when the price breaks out of these average swing levels with sufficient, sustained momentum, filtering out false breakouts by requiring the price to hold a certain percentage distance away from the breakout line.

Slope Direction Forecaster

The "Slope Direction Forecaster" is a trend-following, momentum-based indicator designed to act as an automated "Stop and Reverse" (SAR) system. Its primary goal is to calculate the steepness of the current price trend, filter out "fake" momentum by cross-referencing a higher timeframe, and visually forecast where the trend is heading. Because it normalizes data, it is built to be highly adaptable across different asset classes (Forex, Crypto, Indices) regardless of their inherent price scales or volatility.
